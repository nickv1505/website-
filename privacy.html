Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026

West Coast Finish (“we”, “us”) respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and how we use it, in line with British Columbia’s Personal Information Protection Act (PIPA).

What we collect

  • Contact details you provide, such as your name, phone number, email address and city.
  • Project information, including your message and any photos of your home you choose to upload.

How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, prepare your estimate, schedule and complete work you request, and follow up about that work. We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Photos

Photos you upload are used to understand the scope and condition of your project. We will not publish photos of your home without your permission.

Sharing

We only share your information with service providers that help us operate (for example, email or form-delivery services), and only as needed for those purposes, or where required by law.

Retention & access

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above or as required by law. You may ask to access, correct or delete your personal information at any time by contacting us.
