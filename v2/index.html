    FAQ

    Good questions.

    Something else on your mind? Call (604) 377-9927.

    How much does it cost to paint a house?

    It depends on the size of the space, the condition of the walls, the prep needed and the paint you choose. We give you a free, itemized estimate so you know the cost before anything starts.

    Are estimates really free?

    Yes. Estimates are free and there’s no obligation. Send photos with the form above, or call to book a quick visit.

    Do you paint interiors and exteriors?

    Yes, both: walls, ceilings and doors inside, and stucco, siding, soffits and doors outside.

    How long does a job take?

    A single room usually takes a day or two. A full interior often takes several days to a week, and most exteriors take one to two weeks depending on size and weather.

    Do I need to move my furniture?

    Please clear small and fragile items. We move and cover larger furniture and put it back when we’re done.

    Can you help me choose colours?

    Yes. We bring real samples to your home and help you pick colours that work in your light.

    What areas do you serve?

    Homes across Metro Vancouver and the Lower Mainland, including Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, the North Shore, New Westminster, the Tri-Cities, Surrey, Delta and Langley.
