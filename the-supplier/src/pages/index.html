FAQ

What am I buying?

A digital information package about one supplier: their contact details, website or social links, the product categories they offer, ordering information, and notes on working with them for reselling. You're buying information. It is not inventory, a course, or a membership.

How do I get my supplier?

Right after your payment is confirmed through Stripe, you're taken to a private access page with the full supplier information. Bookmark that page. You can come back to it, copy the details, or save it as a PDF.

Is this a subscription?

No. It's a single payment of $19.99 USD. There are no recurring charges and nothing to cancel.

Can I use the supplier for reselling?

That's what the package is for. Whether a deal works is between you and the supplier: pricing, minimums and terms are set by them and can change. You're responsible for checking their products and following the laws, platform rules and resale requirements where you sell.

Does this guarantee that I'll make money?

No. Supplier information can help you get started, but sales and profit depend on many things outside our control, like your pricing, marketing, platform and market demand. We don't promise any income.

Do you offer refunds?

Because the information is delivered instantly and can't be returned, purchases are generally final once access has been delivered. If you were charged but couldn't access your supplier page, or you were charged twice, contact us and we'll make it right. See our Refund Policy.

Purchasing supplier information does not guarantee sales, profits, or business success. Customers are responsible for evaluating suppliers and complying with applicable laws, platform rules, and resale requirements.
