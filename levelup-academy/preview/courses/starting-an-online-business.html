Starting an Online Business

Find an opportunity, validate demand cheaply, set up your business properly in Canada, and grow carefully.

A step-by-step guide to starting a small online business the careful way. Find and validate opportunities before spending money, understand customers and competitors, build an offer, calculate margins, register and set up a business in Canada, keep proper records, acquire customers and scale responsibly.
