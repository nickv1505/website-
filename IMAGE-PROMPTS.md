# West Coast Finish: AI image prompts

These are ready-to-paste prompts for every photo on the site. Use any AI image tool, such as ChatGPT (image generation), Google Gemini, Midjourney, Adobe Firefly or Ideogram.

## How to use

1. For each image below, paste the prompt, then add the **style line** at the end.
2. Pick the most realistic result. Skip anything with warped furniture, melted objects, extra
   windows, strange text, or anything that looks too perfect.
3. Download it and name it **exactly** as shown (e.g. `hero.jpg`).
4. Upload all files to the `assets/img/photos/` folder on GitHub (open the repo on the
   `claude/trusting-mccarthy-0k2bvb` branch, choose **Add file → Upload files** and type
   `assets/img/photos/` in the path), or send them to Claude.
5. Tell Claude "photos are uploaded" and it will connect them to the site.

You don't need to do them all at once. Any image you haven't made yet keeps its current stock photo.

**Size:** landscape (3:2 or 16:9), at least 1600 px wide. JPG is fine.

### Style line (add to the end of every prompt)

> Photorealistic professional real-estate photography, full-frame camera, 24mm lens, natural
> daylight, true-to-life colours, realistic proportions and textures, subtle natural
> imperfections, tidy but lived-in, no text, no watermarks, no logos.

## 4. Before & After (4 pairs)

These pairs need to show the **same room from the same angle**. The best way to get that:

1. Generate the **after** image first with the prompt below.
2. In the same chat, upload or refer to that image and ask:
   *"Edit this exact image to show the same room before repainting: same camera angle, same
   furniture and layout, but the walls have faded, slightly yellowed, uneven old paint with
   scuff marks, small dents and nail holes, and a few stains. Keep it realistic and subtle,
   not ruined."*
   For the exterior, ask for *"faded, chalky, weathered paint with peeling on the trim and dirty
   siding."*
