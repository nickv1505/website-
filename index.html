    FAQ

    Frequently asked questions

    Contact Us
    How much does it cost to paint a house?

    It depends on the size of the space, the condition of the surfaces, the amount of prep needed, and the paint you choose. Because every home is different, we give you a free, itemized estimate so you know the exact cost before any work starts.

    Do you provide free estimates?

    Yes — estimates are always free and there’s no obligation. You can request one online, send photos, or call us to book a quick visit.

    Do you paint both interiors and exteriors?

    Yes. We handle interior painting (walls, ceilings, doors and trim) as well as exterior painting (siding, trim, fascia, soffits and doors) for residential homes.

    How long does a typical painting project take?

    A single room is usually done in a day or two. A full interior often takes several days to a week, and most exteriors take about one to two weeks depending on size, prep and weather. We’ll give you a timeline with your estimate.

    Do I need to move my furniture?

    We ask that you clear small items, décor and anything fragile. We’ll move larger furniture to the centre of the room, cover it, and put it back when we’re done.

    Do you paint ceilings and trim?

    Yes. Ceilings, baseboards, door frames, window casings and crown moulding are all part of what we do — on their own or together with walls.

    Can I send photos of my home for an estimate?

    Absolutely. Upload photos with the estimate form. Photos help us understand the space and condition — for larger projects we may still book a short visit to confirm details.

    What areas do you serve?

    We serve homeowners throughout the Lower Mainland / Metro Vancouver, including Vancouver, Burnaby, Richmond, the North Shore, New Westminster, the Tri-Cities, Surrey, Delta and Langley. Not sure if you’re in our area? Just ask.
